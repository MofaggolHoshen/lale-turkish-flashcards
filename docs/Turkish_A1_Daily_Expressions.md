# Turkish Daily Expressions & Responses (A1)

This lesson is a clean and readable version of common Turkish daily expressions with English meanings.

---

## 1) Greetings and Daily Salutations

| Turkish | English Meaning |
|---|---|
| Günaydın! | Good morning! |
| İyi günler! | Good day / Good afternoon! |
| İyi akşamlar! | Good evening! |
| İyi geceler! | Good night! |
| Hoşça kal! | Take care! / Goodbye! |
| Güle güle! | Goodbye! / See you! |
| Hoş geldin! | Welcome! (informal) |
| Hoş geldiniz! | Welcome! (formal / plural) |
| Hoş bulduk! | Nice to meet you / Welcome! |

## 2) Politeness and Everyday Courtesy

## 5) Useful Short Phrases for Daily Life

- Merhaba! — Hello!
- Selam! — Hi!
- Nasılsın? — How are you?
- İyiyim, teşekkür ederim. — I’m fine, thank you.
- Ne haber? — What’s up?
- Görüşürüz. — See you.
- Hoşça kal. — Take care.
- Sonra görüşürüz. — See you later.
- Ben Türkçe öğreniyorum. — I am learning Turkish.
- Anlamadım. — I didn’t understand.
- Tekrar eder misiniz? — Could you say it again?
- Lütfen yavaş konuşun. — Please speak slowly.

---

## 6) Quick Vocabulary Notes

- Hoş = nice / pleasant
- Geldin / Geldiniz = came / welcome
- Bulduk = we found / we are pleased
- Teşekkür = thanks
- Ederim = I do / I offer
- Özür = apology
- Dilerim = I ask / I wish
- Şans = luck
- Yolculuk = trip / journey
- Bayram = holiday / festival
- Kutlu = blessed / happy

---

## 7) Example Dialogue

A: Günaydın!  
B: Günaydın!  
A: Nasılsın?  
B: İyiyim, teşekkür ederim. Sen nasılsın?  
A: Ben de iyiyim. Hoşça kal!  
B: Güle güle!

---

## 8) Summary

These expressions are very common in Turkish daily life and help with:

- greetings
- thanking and politeness
- travel and work
- health and wishes
- celebrations and festivals

## 10) Useful Reminder

Practice aloud every day. A1 learners often remember phrases faster when they use them in real-life situations, such as:

- greeting a teacher
- thanking a shopkeeper
- speaking to a classmate
- wishing someone on a special day
